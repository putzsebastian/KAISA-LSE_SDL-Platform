Methods (Automated Workflow)

Overview

Loading isotherms were determined across [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_NAME]] concentration(s) ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ligand level(s) ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using a Cytiva 96-well filter plate on Opentrons OT-2 with UR5 handling and a vacuum manifold. Resin mass per well: [[RESIN_MASS]]. Total volume per well: [[TOTAL_VOLUME]]. Buffer: [[BUFFER]].

Automated sequence
  1. Initial setup and vacuum manifold preparation:
    • UR5 staged receiver plate and vacuum manifold; filter plate positioned for vacuum filtration.
    • Vacuum pump set to [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s, followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
  2. Equilibration cycles (n = [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]]):
    • OT-2 dispensed equilibration buffer ([[BUFFER]]) at [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] per well using custom labware (Cytiva 96-well filter plate).
    • UR5 transferred the filter plate to the vacuum manifold; applied vacuum ([[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s), then vented ([[VENTING_TIME]] s), and returned the plate to the OT-2.
    • Each cycle duration: [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]].
  3. Ligand loading:
    • OT-2 dispensed [[LIGAND_NAME]] solutions (stock [[LIGAND_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]]) across concentrations [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]] in [[REPLICATES]] replicate(s) per condition onto resin mass [[RESIN_MASS]] per well.
    • Incubated at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]]°C with shaking [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] rpm for [[INCUBATION_TIME]].
  4. Salt variation:
    • [[SALT_NAME]] stock ([[SALT_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]]) was used to prepare [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]] conditions (n = [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]]).
  5. Post-incubation filtration and handling:
    • UR5 repositioned plates for vacuum filtration; applied vacuum ([[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s) and vented ([[VENTING_TIME]] s).
    • Workflow included operator pauses for covering/uncovering the filter plate and tip exchange as prompted.
  6. Measurement:
    • UR5 loaded the assay plate into the Tecan Spark; absorbance measured at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
    • Quantification used calibration parameters: slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].
