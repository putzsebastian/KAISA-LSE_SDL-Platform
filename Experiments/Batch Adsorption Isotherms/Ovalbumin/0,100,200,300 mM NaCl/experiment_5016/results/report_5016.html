Methods (Automated Workflow)

Overview

Loading isotherms were determined across 4 NaCl concentration(s) (0;100;200;300 mM) and 7 ligand level(s) (0.2;0.5;1.0;1.5;2;5;7 mg/mL) using a Cytiva 96-well filter plate on Opentrons OT-2 with UR5 handling and a vacuum manifold. Resin mass per well: 7.7. Total volume per well: 300. Buffer: 20 mM NaAc.

Automated sequence
  1. Initial setup and vacuum manifold preparation:
    • UR5 staged receiver plate and vacuum manifold; filter plate positioned for vacuum filtration.
    • Vacuum pump set to 25 for 30 s, followed by venting for 300 s.
  2. Equilibration cycles (n = 3):
    • OT-2 dispensed equilibration buffer (20 mM NaAc) at 300 per well using custom labware (Cytiva 96-well filter plate).
    • UR5 transferred the filter plate to the vacuum manifold; applied vacuum (25 for 30 s), then vented (300 s), and returned the plate to the OT-2.
    • Each cycle duration: 5.
  3. Ligand loading:
    • OT-2 dispensed Ovalbumin solutions (stock 10) across concentrations 0.2;0.5;1.0;1.5;2;5;7 mg/mL in 3 replicate(s) per condition onto resin mass 7.7 per well.
    • Incubated at 25°C with shaking 1300 rpm for 120.
  4. Salt variation:
    • NaCl stock (1000) was used to prepare 0;100;200;300 mM conditions (n = 4).
  5. Post-incubation filtration and handling:
    • UR5 repositioned plates for vacuum filtration; applied vacuum (25 for 30 s) and vented (300 s).
    • Workflow included operator pauses for covering/uncovering the filter plate and tip exchange as prompted.
  6. Measurement:
    • UR5 loaded the assay plate into the Tecan Spark; absorbance measured at 280 nm.
    • Quantification used calibration parameters: slope 0.2264, intercept 0.054.
