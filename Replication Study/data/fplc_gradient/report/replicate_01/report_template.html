3. Methods

3.1 Overview of Automated Workflow
  • The experiment was performed on an ÄKTA Pure chromatography system controlled by an automated workflow.
  • The workflow began with a manual pause step to allow the operator to fill the sample loop ("Manually fill sample loop").
  • After the loop was filled, the run was resumed and the automated ÄKTA protocol was started.
  • Sample detection signals recorded during the run included UV (uv1) and conductivity (cond).
  • Sample data were collected with a sampling time of 1 second.
