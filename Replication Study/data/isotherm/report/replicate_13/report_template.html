Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for ligand [[LIGAND_NAME]] on chromatography resin using [[BUFFER]] at varying salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]). The experiment investigates ligand binding as a function of initial ligand concentration ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and salt concentration to parameterize adsorption behavior under defined conditions.

Methods

Overview

Automated determination of loading isotherms was performed using an integrated robotic platform (Opentrons OT-2, UR5 robot arm, vacuum manifold, and Tecan Spark platereader). A 96-well filter plate containing resin and a 96-well deep-well plate containing liquid phases were used to generate a matrix of ligand and salt conditions.

Automated Workflow Description
  1. Preparation and initial equilibration of resin:
    • The UR5 robot transfers a 96-well deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from storage (Storage2) to the vacuum manifold pump position (Pump_Pos1).
    • The UR5 positions the vacuum manifold on the pump station.
    • A 96-well filter plate ("Filterplate") containing resin ([[RESIN_MASS]] per well) is moved by the UR5 from the Opentrons deck position (OT2_Pos1) to the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • The VacuuPump applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] to filter liquid through the resin bed.
    • A venting step of [[VENTING_TIME]] is performed to release vacuum and allow pressure equilibration.
    • The UR5 returns the Filterplate to the Opentrons OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) for further liquid handling.
  2. Automated equilibration cycles (repeated [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] times):
    • The OT-2 executes a protocol using custom labware definition cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json to dispense equilibration buffer ([[BUFFER]]) with volume [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] into each well.
    • The UR5 moves the Filterplate from OT2_Pos1 to the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • The VacuuPump again applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]].
    • The UR5 returns the Filterplate to OT2_Pos1 to complete the equilibration cycle. Each cycle has an effective duration of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]].
  3. Ligand/salt loading setup:
    • The UR5 removes the vacuum manifold and returns the deep-well plate to storage (Storage2).
    • The OT-2 runs a protocol (custom labware: cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json) to distribute ligand solutions with defined concentration series [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and salt concentrations [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) into the Filterplate wells to a total volume of [[TOTAL_VOLUME]].
    • A user pause is triggered: the Filterplate is covered with foil, non-required labware is removed, fresh 300 µL tips are placed in slot 7, and the trash is emptied as prompted by the system.
    • The OT-2 resumes and completes liquid handling steps for loading, followed by a pause requesting removal of the foil from the Filterplate.
  4. Post-incubation filtration:
    • The UR5 transfers a fresh deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from Storage1 to Pump_Pos1 and positions the vacuum manifold.
    • The Filterplate (containing resin and equilibrated ligand/salt mixtures after incubation at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] with shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]]) is moved by the UR5 to the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • The VacuuPump performs filtration at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]]. The filtrates containing unbound ligand are collected in the deep-well plate.
    • The Filterplate is moved to Storage3, the vacuum manifold is removed, and the deep-well plate with filtrates is transferred to the Opentrons deck position OT2_Pos5.
  5. Preparation of measurement plate:
    • A standard 96-well plate ("WP1") is moved by the UR5 from Storage4 to OT2_Pos1.
    • The OT-2 runs a measurement preparation protocol to transfer defined volumes of filtrate from the deep-well plate (OT2_Pos5) into the measurement plate (WP1) for spectroscopic analysis at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
    • After transfer, the deep-well plate is returned to Storage1 and the measurement plate remains at OT2_Pos1.
  6. Plate reader measurement:
    • The UR5 moves the measurement plate (WP1) to the Tecan Spark platereader.
    • The Tecan Spark is opened, the plate is inserted, and the reader is closed.
    • A measurement protocol is executed on the Tecan Spark (e.g., absorbance at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm).
    • Following measurement, the reader is opened, the UR5 removes the plate and returns it to Storage4, and the reader is closed.
  7. Data processing:
    • Absorbance values are converted to supernatant ligand concentrations using the calibration curve with slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]] and intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].
    • The amount of ligand bound to the resin is calculated from the mass balance (initial minus supernatant concentration) considering the total volume [[TOTAL_VOLUME]] and resin mass [[RESIN_MASS]].
    • Loading isotherms (bound ligand per mass of resin vs. equilibrium concentration) are generated for each salt concentration.
