Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for [[LIGAND_NAME]] binding to the chromatography resin at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using an automated, robot-assisted workflow. The goal is to quantify ligand binding capacity as a function of both ligand and salt concentration to support resin characterization and process development.

Methods

Overview of Automated Workflow
  • Automated liquid handling on an Opentrons OT-2 using custom labware definition for a 96-well filter plate (Cytiva 1 mL filter well plate).
  • Robotic plate and manifold handling using a UR5 robot for transport between storage positions, vacuum manifold, Opentrons deck, and Tecan Spark platereader.
  • Vacuum-assisted filtration using a VacuuPump with controlled pump speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] and pump duration [[PUMP_DURATION]] s, followed by venting steps of [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
  • Online absorbance measurement of filtrates or supernatants using a Tecan Spark platereader at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
Buffers, Resin and Ligand
  • Buffer: [[BUFFER]]
  • Salt: [[SALT_NAME]] with stock concentration [[SALT_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]; working salt concentrations: [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] levels (values: [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]).
  • Resin mass per well: [[RESIN_MASS]]
  • Ligand: [[LIGAND_NAME]] with stock concentration [[LIGAND_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]; working ligand concentrations: [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] levels (values: [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]).
  • Replicates: [[REPLICATES]] per condition.
Equilibration of Resin
  • Resin-containing 96-well deep-well plate positioned by UR5 at the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos1).
  • Vacuum manifold placed and removed by UR5 as required for equilibration and processing steps.
  • Opentrons OT-2 performs automated equilibration cycles using the Cytiva 96-well filter plate labware definition.
  • A total of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles are run, each with an equilibration volume of [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] per well and cycle duration [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]] s.
  • Between each equilibration cycle:
    • UR5 transfers the filter plate between OT-2 and the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • VacuuPump applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s.
    • Utility wait step of [[VENTING_TIME]] s for venting before plate transfer back to OT-2.
Ligand Loading and Incubation
  • After equilibration, the UR5 removes the vacuum manifold and returns plates to storage and OT-2 positions as required.
  • Opentrons OT-2 dispenses ligand solutions at different concentrations [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]] into the equilibrated resin-containing wells according to the loading isotherm design.
  • Total liquid volume per well during loading: [[TOTAL_VOLUME]] per condition.
  • Incubation is performed at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] with orbital shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] to reach binding equilibrium.
Filtration and Collection of Supernatant
  • Following incubation, UR5 transports the deep-well plate and filter plate back to the vacuum manifold positions.
  • VacuuPump applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to separate liquid from resin.
  • Utility wait step of [[VENTING_TIME]] s for venting prior to plate removal.
  • The filter plate is then transferred by UR5 to a storage position or Opentrons deck as specified by the workflow.
Measurement of Ligand Concentration
  • UR5 places a measurement plate (WP1) onto the Opentrons deck and subsequently into the Tecan Spark platereader.
  • Tecan Spark is controlled to open and close the drawer and to run the measurement method.
  • Absorbance of supernatant samples is measured at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
  • Ligand concentrations are calculated using a calibration curve with slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]] and intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].
  • Loaded amount per mass of resin is determined from the difference between initial and equilibrium ligand concentrations, normalized by [[RESIN_MASS]].
