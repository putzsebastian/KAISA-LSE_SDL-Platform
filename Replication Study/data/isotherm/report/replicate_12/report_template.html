Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for [[LIGAND_NAME]] on the selected resin at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using an automated robotic workflow. The aim is to quantify bound ligand per gram of resin across a range of feed concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) under defined buffer conditions ([[BUFFER]]).

Methods

Overview of Automated Workflow

The experiment was executed on an integrated automated platform combining an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handler, a UR5 robotic arm, a vacuum manifold with controlled pump, and a Tecan Spark platereader. The process was designed to perform equilibration, loading, incubation, filtration, and absorbance-based quantification for multiple salt and ligand concentration conditions.

Automated Equilibration of Resin
  • Resin was pre-dispensed into a 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") with [[RESIN_MASS]] resin per well.
  • The UR5 robotic arm transferred the deep-well plate from storage to the vacuum manifold position.
  • A 96-well filter plate ("Filterplate") containing the resin bed was placed on the Opentrons OT-2 deck (custom labware: cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml).
  • The OT-2 executed equilibration protocols, adding equilibration buffer ([[BUFFER]]) with a total volume of [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] per cycle.
  • The UR5 moved the filter plate onto the vacuum manifold, and the vacuum pump was applied at [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] per cycle to drain the wells.
  • After each pump step, a venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] was applied to allow pressure equilibration.
  • The equilibration sequence was repeated for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] cycles, each with a duration of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]].
Automated Ligand Loading and Incubation
  • Following equilibration, the filter plate was returned to the OT-2 deck.
  • The OT-2 prepared and dispensed ligand solutions of [[LIGAND_NAME]] at defined concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) at varying salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) into the equilibrated resin-containing wells, ensuring a total volume of [[TOTAL_VOLUME]] per well.
  • The plate was incubated at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] with shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] to reach adsorption equilibrium.
Filtration and Collection of Flow-Through
  • After incubation, the UR5 transferred the deep-well plate and filter plate to the vacuum manifold.
  • The vacuum pump was again applied at [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] with a subsequent venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] to collect clarified flow-through samples in the receiver plate.
  • The UR5 returned the receiver plate containing the flow-through ("96DeepWP" at OT2_Pos5) to the OT-2 deck for subsequent processing.
Preparation of Measurement Plate and Spectrophotometric Readout
  • The UR5 placed a fresh 96-well plate ("WP1") on the OT-2 deck.
  • The OT-2 transferred defined volumes of flow-through from the receiver deep-well plate into "WP1".
  • The UR5 moved "WP1" into the Tecan Spark platereader.
  • The Tecan Spark performed absorbance measurements at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm using the pre-defined method.
  • After measurement, "WP1" was returned to storage by the UR5.
Data Processing
  • Flow-through absorbance values were converted to equilibrium ligand concentrations using the calibration curve (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]).
  • Loaded ligand per gram of resin was calculated from the mass balance between loaded and residual ligand, normalized by [[RESIN_MASS]] and the total volume [[TOTAL_VOLUME]].
  • Loading isotherms were generated for each salt concentration and fitted to appropriate adsorption models as needed.
