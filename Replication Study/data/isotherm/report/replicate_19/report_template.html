Automated Workflow Overview

The loading isotherm experiment was executed by an automated workflow that coordinated the Opentrons OT-2 liquid handler, UR5 robot, vacuum manifold with pump, and a Tecan Spark platereader. The workflow proceeded as follows:

  1. Initial plate setup and equilibration preparation:
    • The Opentrons OT-2 executed a protocol using the custom labware definition cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json to prepare the 96-well filter plate with resin and buffer.
    • The UR5 robot retrieved a 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from storage and placed it onto the vacuum manifold pump position (Pump_Pos1).
    • The UR5 placed the vacuum manifold onto the pump position.
    • The UR5 transferred the prepared filter plate from the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) onto the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • The vacuum pump was started at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for a duration of [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to drain liquid through the resin bed.
    • After pumping, the system waited for venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] s before the UR5 moved the filter plate back to the OT-2 (OT2_Pos1).
  2. Equilibration cycles:
    • A loop of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles was performed.
    • In each cycle, the OT-2 ran the equilibration protocol (custom labware: cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json) to dispense equilibration buffer (volume per cycle: [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]]).
    • The UR5 moved the filter plate between the OT-2 (OT2_Pos1) and the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos2).
    • The vacuum pump operated at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]] s in each cycle, followed by a venting wait of [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
    • After each cycle, the UR5 returned the filter plate to the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) for the next equilibration step.
  3. Post-equilibration handling:
    • After the final equilibration cycle, the UR5 removed the vacuum manifold and returned the 96-deep-well plate from the pump position to storage.
    • The OT-2 executed a protocol to prepare for ligand loading and user intervention steps.
    • A pause was triggered prompting the user to cover the filter plate with foil, remove all other labware, place fresh 300 µL tips in slot 7, and empty the trash.
    • The OT-2 then ran a further protocol for reagent/ligand handling, followed by a pause requesting foil removal from the filter plate.
  4. Ligand loading and incubation:
    • The UR5 retrieved another 96-deep-well plate from storage and placed it on the pump position, then placed the vacuum manifold.
    • The UR5 transferred the filter plate from the OT-2 to the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • The vacuum pump ran at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] s to remove unbound liquid.
    • The UR5 then moved the filter plate to a storage position, removed the vacuum manifold, and relocated the 96-deep-well plate to the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos5).
    • The UR5 placed a fresh assay plate ("WP1") on the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1).
    • The OT-2 ran the final protocol, transferring supernatant or sample from the deep-well plate to the assay plate according to the defined ligand and salt concentration layout (total volume per well: [[TOTAL_VOLUME]]; incubation time prior to transfer: [[INCUBATION_TIME]]).
  5. Plate reader measurement:
    • After the OT-2 protocol, the UR5 returned the 96-deep-well plate to storage and transferred the assay plate (WP1) from the OT-2 to the Tecan Spark.
    • The Tecan Spark plate reader was closed and the measurement protocol executed at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
    • Following completion, the Tecan Spark opened, the UR5 retrieved the assay plate and returned it to storage.

Ligand concentrations in the supernatant were calculated from the Tecan Spark absorbance readings using the calibration curve (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]). Bound ligand per mass of resin was determined by mass balance, yielding the loading isotherms across the different salt concentrations [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]).
