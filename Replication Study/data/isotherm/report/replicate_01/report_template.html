Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for ligand [[LIGAND_NAME]] on resin using [[RESIN_MASS]] of resin at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) in buffer [[BUFFER]]. The experiment is performed using an automated workflow to ensure reproducibility across [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] salt conditions and [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ligand concentration levels ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]).

Methods

Overview of Automated Workflow

The determination of loading isotherms was carried out using an integrated automated platform consisting of an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handling robot, a UR5 robotic arm, a vacuum manifold with VacuuPump, and a Tecan Spark platereader. The workflow was orchestrated to perform equilibration, ligand loading, filtration, and analytical measurement steps without manual intervention, except at designated pause steps.

Automated Workflow Description
  1. System preparation and plate setup
    • The Opentrons OT-2 was configured with custom labware definition cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml for the 96-well filter plate ("Filterplate").
    • A 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") containing buffer, salt stock dilutions ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]), and ligand solutions ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) was placed in storage location and handled by the UR5 arm.
    • The Tecan Spark platereader was prepared for absorbance measurements at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
  2. Initial equilibration setup
    • The OT-2 executed a protocol using the custom filter plate labware to dispense equilibration buffer [[BUFFER]] with defined salt concentration [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] into the filter plate wells containing [[RESIN_MASS]] of resin.
    • The UR5 robot grabbed the 96DeepWP from storage (Storage2) and placed it on the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos1).
    • The UR5 placed the vacuum manifold and then transferred the filter plate from the OT-2 deck position (OT2_Pos1) to the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos2).
    • The VacuuPump applied vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to pull equilibration buffer through the resin bed, followed by a venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
    • The UR5 returned the filter plate to the OT-2 deck, ready for further liquid handling.
  3. Automated equilibration cycles
    • A loop of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles was executed. In each cycle, the OT-2 dispensed [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] of equilibration buffer with the desired salt concentration(s) onto the resin in the filter plate.
    • After dispensing, the UR5 transferred the filter plate to the vacuum manifold, and the VacuuPump applied vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s, followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
    • The UR5 then moved the filter plate back to the OT-2 deck to complete the cycle.
  4. Post-equilibration handling
    • After completion of the equilibration loop, the UR5 removed the vacuum manifold and returned the 96DeepWP from the vacuum position back to storage.
    • The OT-2 executed an additional protocol step (using the custom labware) to prepare the filter plate and deep-well plate for ligand loading.
    • A user pause was introduced to cover the filter plate with foil and to refresh tips and empty trash, ensuring clean conditions for the loading step.
    • The OT-2 then ran a subsequent protocol after the pause to complete pre-loading operations and a second pause to remove the foil prior to loading.
  5. Ligand loading and incubation
    • The UR5 retrieved a fresh 96DeepWP from storage (Storage1) and placed it onto the vacuum station (Pump_Pos1), and the vacuum manifold was positioned.
    • The UR5 moved the equilibrated filter plate from the OT-2 deck to the vacuum station (Pump_Pos2) for any preparatory filtration steps, followed by vacuum and venting as above.
    • The UR5 then stored the prepared filter plate, and the 96DeepWP containing ligand solutions ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]] at various [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) was transferred by UR5 to the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos5).
    • A standard 96-well microplate ("WP1") was placed by UR5 at OT-2 position 1 for collection of supernatants or post-loading samples.
    • The OT-2 executed a protocol to dispense ligand solutions into the resin-containing filter plate wells (total loading volume [[TOTAL_VOLUME]] per well) and to transfer supernatant or filtrate into WP1 after incubation.
    • Ligand-resin contact occurred at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] with shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] to reach equilibrium binding in each salt condition.
  6. Filtration and sample transfer for analysis
    • Following incubation, filtration was carried out using the vacuum manifold and VacuuPump at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s, with venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] s, to separate liquid phase from the resin.
    • Supernatant or filtrate was collected into the designated plate (WP1), which contained all combinations of [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] salt conditions and [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ligand levels, with [[REPLICATES]] replicates each.
  7. Analytical measurement (Tecan Spark)
    • The UR5 transferred WP1 from the OT-2 deck to the Tecan Spark platereader.
    • The Tecan Spark performed an absorbance measurement at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm to determine ligand concentration in the liquid phase.
    • Calibration curve parameters (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]) were used to convert absorbance to concentration, enabling calculation of bound vs. free ligand and construction of loading isotherms for each salt condition.
    • After measurement, the UR5 returned WP1 to storage.
