Objective

To determine loading isotherms for ligand [[LIGAND_NAME]] binding to the resin using automated liquid handling and robotic workflow at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and ligand concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]).

Methods

Overview: An integrated automated workflow using an Opentrons OT-2, UR5 robotic arm, vacuum manifold, and Tecan Spark platereader was used to perform equilibration, ligand loading, incubation, and measurement for loading isotherm determination.

Automated Workflow Description

Device configuration: The Opentrons OT-2 was configured with custom labware corresponding to a Cytiva 96-well filter plate (1 mL) and deep-well plates for collection. A UR5 robotic arm handled plate transfers between storage positions, the vacuum manifold, the OT-2 deck, and the Tecan Spark platereader. A vacuum pump was used for liquid removal through the filter plate, and a shaker/incubator was used for equilibration and incubation steps.

Equilibration of Resin
  1. The 96-well deep-well plate for waste/collection ("96DeepWP") was moved from storage to the vacuum manifold position by the UR5 robot.
  2. The vacuum manifold was placed onto the deep-well plate.
  3. The resin-containing 96-well filter plate was transferred between the OT-2 deck and the vacuum manifold as required for equilibration.
  4. Equilibration buffer ([[BUFFER]] with [[SALT_NAME]]) was dispensed by the OT-2 into each filter well to a volume of [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] per cycle.
  5. Vacuum was applied using the pump at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] seconds to pull the equilibration buffer through the resin into the underlying deep-well plate.
  6. After each vacuum step, the system waited for [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds to vent the manifold before moving plates.
  7. Steps 4-6 were repeated for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles, with each cycle duration of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]] seconds.
  8. After equilibration, the vacuum manifold was removed and the deep-well plate returned to storage.
Ligand Loading and Incubation
  1. The OT-2 dispensed ligand [[LIGAND_NAME]] solutions at different concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) into the equilibrated resin filter plate.
  2. Total loading volume per well was [[TOTAL_VOLUME]], distributed according to the planned design over [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ligand levels and [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] salt conditions with [[REPLICATES]] replicates each.
  3. The plate was incubated at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] with shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] to reach binding equilibrium.
  4. After incubation, the filter plate and a receiving deep-well plate were positioned on the vacuum manifold, and vacuum was applied at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] seconds to collect the flow-through.
  5. The system again waited [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds for venting before moving plates back to storage or the OT-2 deck.
Preparation for Measurement
  1. The deep-well plate containing the ligand solutions (pre- or post-binding, depending on the protocol design) was transferred by the UR5 to the OT-2 deck.
  2. The OT-2 transferred defined volumes to a measurement plate (WP1) suitable for the Tecan Spark platereader.
  3. Any required user interventions (e.g., covering/uncovering plates with foil, refreshing tips, emptying trash) were prompted via pauses in the automated workflow.
  4. The UR5 moved the measurement plate to the Tecan Spark, which was opened and closed automatically by the instrument for the measurement sequence.
Spectroscopic Measurement and Calculation of Bound Ligand
  1. The Tecan Spark measured absorbance at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm for all wells.
  2. A pre-determined calibration curve (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]) was used to convert absorbance values to ligand concentrations.
  3. The amount of ligand bound to the resin per well was calculated from the difference between initial and equilibrium ligand concentrations, taking into account [[TOTAL_VOLUME]] and resin mass [[RESIN_MASS]].
  4. Loading isotherms were then constructed as bound ligand per mass of resin vs. equilibrium ligand concentration for each salt condition.
