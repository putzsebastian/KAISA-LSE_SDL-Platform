Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for the ligand [[LIGAND_NAME]] on the resin at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using an automated robotic workflow. The study investigates the impact of salt concentration ([[SALT_NAME]]) and ligand concentration ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) on binding capacity under controlled incubation and equilibration conditions.

Automated Robotic Workflow Description
  1. Preparation and Setup
    • The Opentrons OT-2 is configured with a custom Cytiva 96-well filter plate labware definition (cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json).
    • The UR5 robot retrieves a 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from storage and places it on the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos1).
    • The UR5 places the vacuum manifold onto the pump position and transfers the filter plate from the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) to the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos2).
    • The VacuuPump applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] to equilibrate or filter the resin-containing wells, followed by a venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] to release vacuum.
  2. Automated Equilibration Cycles
    • A loop of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles is executed using eLabFTW iteration control.
    • In each cycle, the OT-2 runs a protocol with the custom filter plate labware to dispense equilibration buffer ([[BUFFER]]) with volume [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] into the filter plate.
    • The UR5 transfers the filter plate from the OT-2 position to the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • The VacuuPump applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] to remove the equilibration buffer.
    • The UR5 returns the filter plate to the OT-2 position (OT2_Pos1) to start the next equilibration cycle.
  3. Post-Equilibration Handling
    • After completion of all equilibration cycles, the UR5 removes the vacuum manifold and returns the 96-deep-well plate to storage.
    • The OT-2 runs an additional protocol to dispense ligand solutions at defined concentrations [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] and salt concentrations [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] into the equilibrated resin-containing wells (in a Cytiva 96-well filter plate).
    • A user pause is triggered to cover the filter plate with foil, refresh pipette tips, and clear the deck as required.
  4. Ligand Loading and Incubation
    • The OT-2 executes a protocol to add ligand solutions from a working plate into the filter plate containing resin, following the design matrix of ligand and salt concentrations.
    • The plate is incubated for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] with shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] to allow ligand binding to the resin.
  5. Post-Incubation Filtration
    • The UR5 transfers a 96-deep-well plate containing collection wells to the vacuum station and places the vacuum manifold.
    • The UR5 moves the incubated filter plate onto the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2).
    • The VacuuPump applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] to collect the flow-through and supernatant in the deep-well plate.
    • The filter plate is then moved to storage, and the vacuum manifold is removed. The deep-well plate is transferred to the OT-2 deck for subsequent processing if needed.
  6. Preparation of Measurement Plate
    • The UR5 places a standard 96-well plate ("WP1") on the OT-2 deck.
    • The OT-2 runs a protocol to transfer defined volumes from the deep-well plate to the measurement plate (WP1), possibly including dilution steps to fit into the linear range defined by the calibration curve (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]).
  7. Spectrophotometric Measurement
    • The UR5 transports the filled measurement plate (WP1) to the Tecan Spark plate reader.
    • The Tecan Spark closes the chamber and runs the measurement at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm to quantify the concentration of unbound ligand in each well.
    • After measurement, the UR5 retrieves the plate from the Tecan Spark and returns it to storage.
