Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for [[LIGAND_NAME]] onto the chromatographic resin at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using an automated robotic workflow. The binding behavior is quantified using a calibration curve with slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]] and intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].

Automated Workflow Description

The loading isotherm experiment is executed on an integrated robotic platform combining an Opentrons OT-2 pipetting robot, a UR5 robotic arm, a vacuum manifold with pump, and a Tecan Spark plate reader. The workflow proceeds as follows:

  1. Initial equilibration setup
    • The Opentrons OT-2 prepares the filter plate (custom labware file: cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json) with resin and equilibration buffer.
    • The UR5 robot retrieves a 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from Storage2 and places it at the vacuum station (Pump_Pos1).
    • The UR5 places the vacuum manifold onto the deep-well plate at the pump position.
    • The UR5 moves the prepared filter plate from the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) onto the vacuum manifold at Pump_Pos2.
    • The vacuum pump is activated at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] seconds to pull liquid through the resin bed.
    • After pumping, the system vents for [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds to release vacuum and allow complete drainage.
    • The UR5 returns the filter plate to the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) for further processing.
  2. Equilibration cycles
    • An automated loop of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles is performed.
    • In each cycle, the OT-2 dispenses equilibration buffer (volume per cycle: [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]]) into each well of the filter plate.
    • The UR5 transfers the filter plate between the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) and the vacuum station (Pump_Pos2).
    • The vacuum pump is run at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]] seconds, followed by a venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds.
    • After each cycle, the UR5 returns the filter plate to the OT-2 deck to start the next equilibration step.
  3. Post-equilibration handling
    • After the final equilibration cycle, the UR5 removes the vacuum manifold and returns the 96DeepWP from Pump_Pos1 back to Storage2.
    • The OT-2 performs an additional protocol step on the equilibrated filter plate (e.g., buffer exchange or pre-loading operations) using the custom filter plate labware.
    • A manual pause is triggered: the user covers the filter plate with foil, removes all other labware from the OT-2, loads fresh 300 µL tips in Slot 7, and empties trash.
    • The OT-2 runs the next protocol, followed by a second pause for removal of the foil from the filter plate.
  4. Ligand loading at different salt concentrations
    • The UR5 positions a fresh 96DeepWP at the vacuum station (Pump_Pos1) and installs the vacuum manifold.
    • The UR5 moves the filter plate from the OT-2 deck to Pump_Pos2.
    • The vacuum pump is run again at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] seconds and vented for [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds to remove residual liquid.
    • The UR5 transfers the filter plate to Storage3 for temporary storage.
    • The vacuum manifold is removed, and the 96DeepWP is placed onto the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos5).
    • The UR5 loads a fresh microplate ("WP1") from Storage4 onto OT2_Pos1.
    • The OT-2 executes a protocol that dispenses ligand solutions with defined concentrations [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] and salt concentrations [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] into the resin-containing filter wells, creating the loading matrix for isotherm determination.
  5. Post-loading handling and transfer for measurement
    • After incubation for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] (on the shaker at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]]), the UR5 transfers the 96DeepWP containing filtrates from OT2_Pos5 back to Storage1.
    • The UR5 moves the microplate WP1 (containing samples for analysis) from OT2_Pos1 to the Tecan Spark plate reader.
    • The Tecan Spark is opened and closed as needed, and an automated measurement run is started, recording absorbance at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm for each well.
    • After measurement, the UR5 returns the plate WP1 to Storage4 for storage or further processing.

The measured absorbance values are converted to ligand concentrations using the calibration curve (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]), and the bound ligand per mass of resin is calculated to construct loading isotherms as a function of [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] and [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]].
