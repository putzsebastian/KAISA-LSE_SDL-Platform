Automated Workflow Overview

The loading isotherm experiment was executed using an integrated automated platform consisting of an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handler, a UR5 robotic arm, a vacuum manifold with pump, and a Tecan Spark platereader. The workflow was fully scripted and repeated for multiple equilibration cycles and salt conditions as summarized below.

1. Resin Packing and Initial Equilibration Setup
  • The Opentrons OT-2 ran a protocol using a custom Cytiva 96-well filter plate labware definition (cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml) to dispense resin slurry and buffer into the filter plate according to the desired resin mass ([[RESIN_MASS]]) and total volume ([[TOTAL_VOLUME]]).
  • The UR5 robotic arm retrieved a 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from storage and placed it on the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos1).
  • The UR5 placed the vacuum manifold on the pump and then transferred the prepared filter plate from the OT-2 deck position (OT2_Pos1) to the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos2).
  • The vacuum pump was actuated at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] (setpoint 25) for [[PUMP_DURATION]] seconds (setpoint 30 s) to pull liquid through the resin bed, followed by a venting step of [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds (setpoint 300 s) to release vacuum.
  • The UR5 returned the equilibrated filter plate to the OT-2 deck for subsequent processing.

2. Automated Equilibration Cycles

The equilibration of the resin was performed in an automated loop for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] cycles, with each cycle corresponding to an eLabFTW-documented iteration.

  • For each equilibration cycle i (1 to [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]]):
    • The OT-2 ran the equilibration protocol with custom labware to dispense [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] of equilibration buffer ([[BUFFER]] with [[SALT_NAME]] at the current condition) into each well.
    • The UR5 transferred the filter plate from the OT-2 deck to the vacuum manifold position.
    • The vacuum pump was run at [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]] seconds (nominal setpoint 30 s) to remove the equilibration buffer, followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds.
    • The UR5 returned the filter plate to the OT-2 deck to start the next equilibration cycle.

3. Post-Equilibration Handling and Plate Preparation
  • After the final equilibration cycle, the UR5 removed the vacuum manifold and returned the deep-well plate to storage.
  • The OT-2 performed a protocol step to dispense ligands and buffers according to the experimental design for [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ligand concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) across [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] salt concentrations.
  • A pause was introduced for manual intervention to cover the filter plate with foil and prepare fresh tips and empty trash as prompted by the platform.
  • The OT-2 then executed an incubation/dispensing protocol while the filter plate was covered, followed by a second pause to remove the foil before proceeding.

4. Loading / Incubation Step
  • The UR5 placed a fresh 96-deep-well plate on the vacuum manifold and re-mounted the vacuum manifold.
  • The filter plate containing equilibrated resin and ligand solutions was transferred to the vacuum manifold.
  • After incubation for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] with shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]], the vacuum pump step (speed [[PUMP_SPEED]], duration [[PUMP_DURATION]]) was used to collect the flow-through into the deep-well plate, followed by venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds.
  • The filter plate was then moved to storage, and the manifold and collection plate were reconfigured so that the 96-deep-well flow-through plate could be transferred to the OT-2 and then to the appropriate deck position for subsequent handling.

5. Transfer to Assay Plate and Measurement
  • The UR5 placed a fresh assay plate ("WP1") on the OT-2 deck (position OT2_Pos1).
  • The OT-2 executed a protocol to transfer flow-through samples from the deep-well plate into the assay plate, maintaining the mapping of salt and ligand concentration conditions.
  • The UR5 moved the deep-well plate back to storage and transferred the assay plate (WP1) from the OT-2 deck to the Tecan Spark platereader.
  • The Tecan Spark was closed and the programmed measurement was started, using a wavelength of [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm for ligand quantification based on a pre-determined calibration curve.
  • After completion, the plate was retrieved by the UR5 and returned to storage.

6. Data Processing
  • Absorbance readings at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm were converted to ligand concentrations in the flow-through using the calibration curve with slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]] and intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].
  • Ligand bound to resin was calculated from the initial ligand concentration ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]]) and the measured flow-through concentration, normalized by resin mass ([[RESIN_MASS]]).
  • Loading isotherms were constructed for each salt concentration ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and ligand concentration condition.
