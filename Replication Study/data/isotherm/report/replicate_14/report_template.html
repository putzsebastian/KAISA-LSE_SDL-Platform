Experiment Overview

This experiment determines loading isotherms of the ligand [[LIGAND_NAME]] on the resin at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using an automated, robot-assisted workflow. Binding capacities are evaluated across [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] salt conditions ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]]) and [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ligand concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) with [[REPLICATES]] replicate measurements per condition.

The resin mass per well is [[RESIN_MASS]], equilibrated with [[BUFFER]] containing the salt [[SALT_NAME]]. Total process volume per well is [[TOTAL_VOLUME]]. Ligand stock concentration is [[LIGAND_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]] and salt stock concentration is [[SALT_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]].

Ligand loading is quantified via absorbance measurements at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm using a pre-established calibration curve with slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]] and intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].

Methods

Automated Workflow Description

The loading isotherm experiment is executed on an integrated automated platform comprising an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handler, a UR5 robotic arm, a vacuum manifold with pump, and a Tecan Spark platereader. The workflow is summarized as follows:

1. Resin Loading and Initial Setup
  • The Opentrons OT-2 runs a protocol configured for a Cytiva 96-well filter plate (labware file: cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json) to dispense resin slurry into the filter plate wells (resin mass per well: [[RESIN_MASS]]).
  • The UR5 robot transfers a 96-deepwell plate ("96DeepWP") from storage (Storage2) to the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos1).
  • The UR5 places the vacuum manifold onto the deepwell plate (Pump_Pos1).
  • The UR5 transfers the prepared filter plate from the OT-2 position (OT2_Pos1) onto the vacuum manifold (Pump_Pos2), assembling the filter plate-manifold-deepwell stack.
2. Vacuum Filtration and Venting
  • The vacuum pump is activated at a speed of [[PUMP_SPEED]] for a duration of [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to filter liquid through the resin bed.
  • After pumping, the system waits for [[VENTING_TIME]] s for venting and pressure equilibration.
  • The UR5 then returns the filter plate from Pump_Pos2 to OT2_Pos1 to allow further liquid handling steps.
3. Resin Equilibration Cycles

Resin is equilibrated with buffer [[BUFFER]] containing salt [[SALT_NAME]] to a defined equilibration volume of [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] per cycle. A total of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles are performed, each with a cycle duration of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]]:

  • Within a loop over the Equilibration Cycles, the OT-2 dispenses equilibration buffer (volume per cycle: [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]]) into the filter plate using the custom Cytiva filter plate labware definition.
  • The UR5 transfers the filter plate from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2 on the vacuum manifold-deepwell stack.
  • The vacuum pump is run at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to pull the equilibration buffer through the resin.
  • A venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] s is applied before the UR5 returns the filter plate to OT2_Pos1.
  • This sequence is repeated for all [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] cycles to ensure consistent resin equilibration at the target salt conditions.
4. Post-Equilibration Handling
  • After the final equilibration cycle, the UR5 removes the vacuum manifold from the deepwell plate and returns the deepwell plate from Pump_Pos1 to storage (Storage2).
  • The OT-2 executes an additional protocol (using the same Cytiva filter plate labware) to prepare the system for ligand loading and to condition the resin as required.
  • A user pause is triggered with instructions: "Cover Filterplate with Foil. Remove all other labware. Fresh 300 μL tips in Slot 7. Empty Trash." The operator performs the manual steps and resumes the run.
  • The OT-2 runs a further protocol step, followed by a second pause instructing: "Remove Foil from Filterplate" before proceeding.
5. Ligand Loading at Different Salt Concentrations
  • The UR5 moves a fresh 96-deepwell plate from Storage1 to Pump_Pos1 and places the vacuum manifold onto it.
  • The filter plate containing equilibrated resin is moved from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2 to reassemble the filtration stack.
  • The vacuum pump is again operated at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s followed by a venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
  • The UR5 transfers the filter plate to a storage location (Storage3), removes the vacuum manifold, and places the deepwell plate onto OT2_Pos5.
  • A fresh assay plate ("WP1") is moved from Storage4 to OT2_Pos1.
  • The OT-2 runs a ligand loading and sampling protocol, dispensing ligand solutions of varying concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) onto the resin and/or into the assay plate as designed. The samples are incubated at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]]°C with shaker speed [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] for an incubation time of [[INCUBATION_TIME]].
  • After incubation and any required transfers, the UR5 returns the deepwell plate from OT2_Pos5 to Storage1.
6. Plate Reading and Data Acquisition
  • The UR5 grabs the assay plate "WP1" from OT2_Pos1 and transfers it into the Tecan Spark platereader.
  • The Tecan Spark is opened and closed by the system, and a measurement run is executed to record absorbance at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm for all wells corresponding to the different combinations of ligand and salt concentration.
  • After measurement, the UR5 retrieves the plate from the Tecan Spark and returns it to Storage4. The Tecan lid is closed, completing the automated run.
