Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for the ligand [[LIGAND_NAME]] on the resin at different salt concentrations [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using an automated workflow integrating Opentrons OT-2, UR5 robot, vacuum manifold, and Tecan Spark platereader.

Methods

Overview

Loading isotherms were determined by equilibrating a filter plate packed with resin with buffer containing defined salt concentrations, followed by incubation with ligand solutions of varying concentrations. After incubation, filtrates were collected and analyzed spectroscopically to quantify unbound ligand, allowing calculation of resin-bound ligand as a function of equilibrium concentration.

Automated Workflow Description
  1. Initial plate setup on Opentrons OT-2
    • The OT-2 runs a protocol using the custom labware definition cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json to prepare the filter plate (Filterplate) with resin distributed in the wells (resin mass: [[RESIN_MASS]] per well) and to add initial buffer and salt solutions according to the planned matrix of [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] and [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]].
    • The UR5 robot grabs a 96 deep-well plate (96DeepWP) from Storage2 and places it at Pump_Pos1 to act as the filtrate collection plate.
    • The UR5 places the vacuum manifold onto the deep-well plate collection position.
    • The UR5 transfers the prepared filter plate from the OT-2 deck position OT2_Pos1 to the vacuum manifold position Pump_Pos2.
    • The VacuuPump applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to pass liquid through the filter plate into the deep-well collection plate.
    • The system vents for [[VENTING_TIME]] s to release residual vacuum, after which the UR5 returns the filter plate to OT2_Pos1.
  2. Equilibration cycles
    • An equilibration loop is executed for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] cycles, each with an equilibration volume of [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] and duration [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]].
    • In each cycle, the OT-2 dispenses equilibration buffer containing the target salt concentration into the filter plate according to the labware definition.
    • The UR5 transfers the filter plate from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2, where the VacuuPump again applies vacuum at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s, followed by a venting period of [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
    • After each filtration step, the UR5 returns the filter plate to OT2_Pos1, completing one equilibration cycle.
  3. Post-equilibration handling and preparation for ligand loading
    • After the equilibration loop, the UR5 removes the vacuum manifold and returns the deep-well plate from Pump_Pos1 to Storage2.
    • The OT-2 executes an additional protocol with the custom filter plate labware to prepare the plate for ligand loading, for example addition of fresh buffer or pre-incubation steps as defined in the specific protocol.
    • A pause step is used to allow manual intervention: the user is prompted to cover the filter plate with foil, remove all other labware, ensure fresh 300 uL tips in Slot 7, and empty the waste.
    • After resuming, the OT-2 runs a further protocol step, for example ligand addition or mixing, followed by a second pause instructing the user to remove the foil from the filter plate.
  4. Preparation of incubation plate and ligand loading
    • The UR5 retrieves a 96 deep-well plate from Storage1 and places it at Pump_Pos1; the vacuum manifold is placed on top.
    • The UR5 transfers the equilibrated filter plate from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2 and the VacuuPump performs another filtration step at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s with venting for [[VENTING_TIME]] s, ensuring the resin is at the desired buffer and salt conditions.
    • The UR5 then places the filter plate into Storage3 while the vacuum manifold is removed and the deep-well plate is moved to OT2_Pos5 for subsequent use as an incubation or collection plate.
    • The UR5 grabs an additional plate WP1 from Storage4 and places it on OT2_Pos1, after which the OT-2 runs a protocol, for example dispensing ligand solutions of varying concentrations [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] into the incubation or collection plate and or into the filter plate.
  5. Incubation and analytical measurement
    • Ligand and resin incubation is carried out at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] with shaking at [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] for [[INCUBATION_TIME]] as specified in the OT-2 protocol and incubation schedule.
    • Following incubation, the UR5 returns the deep-well plate from OT2_Pos5 to Storage1 and transfers the measurement plate WP1 from OT2_Pos1 to the Tecan Spark.
    • The Tecan Spark is opened, WP1 is placed by the UR5, the reader is closed, and a measurement run is performed at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
    • After the measurement, the Tecan Spark opens, the UR5 removes WP1, the reader is closed, and WP1 is returned to Storage4.
  6. Data processing and loading isotherm calculation
    • Absorbance values measured at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm are converted to ligand concentrations using the calibration curve with slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]] and intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].
    • For each condition defined by salt concentration, ligand feed concentration, and replicate number, the equilibrium ligand concentration in the supernatant is calculated.
    • The amount of ligand bound to the resin per unit mass is determined from the mass balance, that is initial ligand amount minus ligand remaining in the supernatant, normalized by [[RESIN_MASS]].
