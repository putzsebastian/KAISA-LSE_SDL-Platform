Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for [[LIGAND_NAME]] binding to chromatography resin at varying salt concentrations [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] ([[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]), using an automated, integrated workflow for equilibration, loading, washing, and online quantification.

Methods

The experiment was executed on an automated platform combining an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handling robot, a UR5 robotic arm, a vacuum filtration manifold with controlled vacuum pump, and a Tecan Spark plate reader. The workflow used a 96-well filter plate format and deep-well plates for sample and filtrate collection.

Automated Workflow Description
  1. Initial equilibration preparation
    • The Opentrons OT-2 executes a protocol using the custom Cytiva 96-well 1 mL filter plate labware (file: cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json) to distribute equilibration buffer [[BUFFER]] with salt concentration according to [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] into the filter plate and associated deep-well plates.
    • The UR5 robotic arm grabs a 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from Storage2 and places it on the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos1).
    • The UR5 places the vacuum manifold onto the pump position and transfers the equilibrated filter plate from the OT-2 deck position (OT2_Pos1) to the vacuum manifold position (Pump_Pos2).
    • The vacuum pump (VacuuPump) is activated at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to pull the liquid through the resin bed into the deep-well plate.
    • After pumping, the system waits for [[VENTING_TIME]] s to vent the manifold before the UR5 returns the filter plate to OT2_Pos1.
  2. Equilibration cycles
    • A loop of [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] equilibration cycles is executed. For each cycle, the OT-2 dispenses equilibration buffer [[BUFFER]] (volume per cycle [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]]) into all wells of the filter plate.
    • The UR5 transfers the filter plate from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2 above the deep-well plate at Pump_Pos1.
    • The vacuum pump runs at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to remove the equilibration buffer through the resin.
    • The system waits for [[VENTING_TIME]] s ("Venting") to allow pressure equilibration.
    • The UR5 returns the filter plate to OT2_Pos1, rehoming as specified, ready for the next equilibration cycle.
  3. Post-equilibration handling
    • After the final equilibration cycle, the UR5 removes the vacuum manifold and returns the initial 96-deep-well plate to Storage2.
  4. Ligand loading and incubation
    • The OT-2 runs a protocol (custom labware file: cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json) to dispense ligand solutions prepared from [[LIGAND_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]] across the range [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] in buffer [[BUFFER]] with specified salt concentrations [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]].
    • A pause is triggered instructing the operator to cover the filter plate with foil, remove non-required labware, load fresh 300 µL tips into Slot 7, and empty the trash.
    • The OT-2 then performs mixing steps and/or additional liquid handling steps (as defined in the protocol) and a further pause instructs removal of the foil from the filter plate.
    • The UR5 prepares a collection 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from Storage1 to Pump_Pos1 and places the vacuum manifold on the pump.
    • The UR5 transfers the loaded filter plate from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2.
    • The vacuum pump runs at speed [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] s to collect the post-loading filtrate, followed by a venting wait of [[VENTING_TIME]] s.
    • The UR5 moves the processed filter plate to Storage3 and removes the vacuum manifold. The deep-well plate containing filtrate is then transferred to OT2_Pos5 for any subsequent OT-2 operations (e.g., dilution or transfer to an assay plate).
  5. Measurement plate preparation and readout
    • The UR5 retrieves a measurement plate ("WP1") from Storage4 and places it on OT2_Pos1.
    • The OT-2 runs a protocol to transfer aliquots of filtrate from the deep-well plate at OT2_Pos5 into the measurement plate (WP1), thereby mapping the different [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] and [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] to specific wells.
    • After completion, the UR5 returns the deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from OT2_Pos5 to Storage1.
    • The UR5 grabs the filled measurement plate (WP1) from OT2_Pos1 and hands it to the Tecan Spark: the Spark is opened, the plate is placed by the UR5, then the Spark is closed.
    • The Tecan Spark runs the absorbance/fluorescence measurement at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm according to the instrument method.
    • After measurement, the Spark opens again, the UR5 removes WP1, the Spark is closed, and the plate is returned to Storage4.
  6. Data processing
    • Measured signals are converted to ligand concentrations using the calibration curve (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]).
    • Bound ligand per mass of resin is determined from the difference between loaded and filtrate concentrations, using [[RESIN_MASS]], [[TOTAL_VOLUME]], and the mapping of [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] and [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]].
    • Loading isotherms are constructed for each salt condition, summarizing the equilibrium binding of [[LIGAND_NAME]] to the resin.

Summary

The automated workflow successfully determined loading isotherms for [[LIGAND_NAME]] binding to the resin across [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] salt conditions and [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] ligand concentration levels. Quantification via Tecan Spark at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm and calibration parameters (slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]], intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]]) enabled conversion of measured signals to concentrations, from which bound ligand per resin mass [[RESIN_MASS]] was derived. The resulting isotherms provide a basis for selecting suitable operating conditions for loading and for further mechanistic or model-based analysis.
