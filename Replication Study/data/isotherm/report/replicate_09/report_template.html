Objective

Determination of loading isotherms for [[LIGAND_NAME]] binding to the chromatography resin at different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) using an automated high-throughput workflow.

Methods

The experiment was executed on an integrated robotic platform comprising an Opentrons OT-2 liquid handling robot, a UR5 robotic arm, a vacuum manifold with vacuum pump, and a Tecan Spark platereader. The workflow was fully automated according to the following sequence.

Buffer, Ligand, and Resin Setup
  • Resin: Packed into a 96-well filter plate (1 mL filter well plate); resin mass per well: [[RESIN_MASS]].
  • Ligand: [[LIGAND_NAME]] stock solution at [[LIGAND_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]] (working concentration range: [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]).
  • Buffer: [[BUFFER]] with salt [[SALT_NAME]]; salt stock concentration: [[SALT_STOCK_CONCENTRATION]]; target salt conditions: [[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]] (total of [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] distinct salt conditions).
  • Total number of ligand conditions: [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]].
  • Total processed volume per well in equilibration/loading steps: [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] (per cycle) and total loading volume [[TOTAL_VOLUME]].
Automated Workflow Description
  1. Initial OT-2 protocol (filter plate and resin preparation):
    • The Opentrons OT-2 executed a protocol using custom labware definition cytiva_96_filterwellplate_1ml.json to prepare and position the 96-well filter plate containing the resin on the deck (OT-2 position 1).
  2. Transfer of deep-well plate to vacuum manifold station:
    • The UR5 robotic arm grabbed a 96-deep-well plate ("96DeepWP") from storage (Storage2) and placed it at the vacuum station (Pump_Pos1) underneath the vacuum manifold.
    • The UR5 placed the vacuum manifold on top of the deep-well plate to collect filtrate from the filter plate.
  3. Positioning the filter plate on the vacuum manifold:
    • The UR5 grabbed the resin-containing filter plate from the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos1) and placed it onto the vacuum manifold at Pump_Pos2.
  4. Vacuum filtration cycle:
    • The vacuum pump was activated at a speed of [[PUMP_SPEED]] for a duration of [[PUMP_DURATION]] seconds to pull liquid through the filter plate into the underlying deep-well plate.
    • After pumping, the system waited for [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds to allow venting and pressure equilibration.
  5. Return of the filter plate to the OT-2:
    • The UR5 grabbed the filter plate from Pump_Pos2 and returned it to OT2_Pos1 to allow further liquid handling steps on the OT-2.
  6. Equilibration cycles (looped operations):
    • The equilibration of the resin with buffer containing [[SALT_NAME]] was performed for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLES]] automated cycles, each managed via an eLabFTW-linked loop variable.
    • For each equilibration cycle, the OT-2 executed the custom labware protocol to dispense [[EQUILIBRATION_VOLUME]] of equilibration buffer into the resin-containing wells.
    • The UR5 transferred the filter plate between the OT-2 deck and the vacuum manifold (from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2 and back).
    • The vacuum pump was run at [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[EQUILIBRATION_CYCLE_DURATION]] seconds to remove buffer, followed by a venting wait of [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds.
    • After each cycle, the filter plate was returned to OT2_Pos1 for the next automated liquid handling step.
  7. Removal of first deep-well plate and vacuum manifold:
    • The UR5 removed the vacuum manifold and returned the 96-deep-well plate from Pump_Pos1 to Storage2 after equilibration.
  8. Ligand loading preparation on OT-2:
    • The OT-2 ran a protocol (using the same custom filter plate labware) to distribute [[LIGAND_NAME]] solutions across the filter plate. The ligand solutions covered [[NUMBER_OF_LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] different concentrations ([[LIGAND_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[LIGAND_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]) and [[NUMBER_OF_SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] different salt concentrations ([[SALT_CONCENTRATIONS]] [[SALT_CONCENTRATION_UNIT]]).
    • The system was paused to allow manual actions: covering the filter plate with foil, ensuring fresh 300 µL tips in slot 7, and emptying the waste.
    • A further OT-2 protocol was run to apply and mix the ligand solutions with the equilibrated resin (total loading volume per well: [[TOTAL_VOLUME]]).
    • The protocol included an incubation step of [[INCUBATION_TIME]] at [[INCUBATION_TEMPERATURE]] with shaker speed [[SHAKER_SPEED_INCUBATION]] to reach adsorption equilibrium.
    • The user was prompted to remove the foil before proceeding.
  9. Collection of post-loading filtrate:
    • The UR5 retrieved a fresh 96-deep-well plate from Storage1 and positioned it at Pump_Pos1.
    • The vacuum manifold was placed back onto the deep-well plate, and the UR5 transferred the filter plate from OT2_Pos1 to Pump_Pos2.
    • The vacuum pump was activated at [[PUMP_SPEED]] for [[PUMP_DURATION]] seconds to collect the post-loading supernatant in the deep-well plate, followed by a venting wait of [[VENTING_TIME]] seconds.
    • The UR5 then transferred the filter plate to Storage3 and removed the vacuum manifold.
  10. Preparation of measurement plate on OT-2:
    • The UR5 placed the deep-well plate containing collected supernatants from Pump_Pos1 onto the OT-2 deck (OT2_Pos5).
    • A fresh 96-well measurement plate ("WP1") was retrieved from Storage4 and placed at OT2_Pos1.
    • The OT-2 executed a final liquid handling protocol to transfer appropriate aliquots of the supernatant from the deep-well plate (OT2_Pos5) to the measurement plate (OT2_Pos1) for spectrophotometric analysis at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm.
  11. Measurement of ligand concentration on Tecan Spark:
    • The UR5 grabbed the filled measurement plate (WP1) from OT2_Pos1.
    • The Tecan Spark plate reader was opened, the UR5 placed WP1 in the reader, and the reader was closed.
    • A measurement protocol was run on the Tecan Spark to record absorbance (or fluorescence) at [[MEASUREMENT_WAVELENGTH]] nm for each well.
    • After completion, the reader was opened, the UR5 removed the plate from the Tecan position, the reader was closed, and the UR5 returned the plate to storage (Storage4).
  12. Data processing:
    • Ligand concentrations in the supernatant were calculated from the measured signals using a pre-determined calibration curve with slope [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_SLOPE]] and intercept [[CALIBRATION_CURVE_INTERCEPT]].
    • The adsorbed amount of [[LIGAND_NAME]] on the resin was computed from the difference between loaded and equilibrium ligand concentrations, using the total loading volume [[TOTAL_VOLUME]] and resin mass [[RESIN_MASS]].
    • Technical replicates: [[REPLICATES]].
